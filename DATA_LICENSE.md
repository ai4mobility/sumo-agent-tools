# Data license

The files in `fowler/` are derived from OpenStreetMap data.

- `fowler/fowler.osm.xml` is an extract from OpenStreetMap, downloaded on Nov 3, 2026 from api.openstreetmap.org
  (bounding box -82.434, 28.049, -82.399, 28.061).
- `fowler/fowler.net.xml` and the route, detector and count files built from it by `build_fowler.py` are derived
  from that extract. The traffic demand and counts are synthetic.
